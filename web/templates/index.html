About the Project

Social Media Threat Intelligence System is an AI-based application designed to analyze English social-media content and identify potentially threatening or malicious linguistic patterns.

ℹ️

The system is designed as a threat-intelligence classification prototype. It identifies patterns learned during model training and does not independently verify whether information is factually true or whether a person is actually dangerous.
